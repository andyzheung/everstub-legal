Privacy Policy

For App Store version 1.0 · Effective October 18, 2026

Support 简体中文

In short: Everstub does not require an account and stores records on your device first. If you enable iCloud, data syncs only through your private iCloud data space. The developer does not receive, sell, or track your record data.

1. Information used by Everstub

Images and PDFs that you capture, select, or import, plus record fields you enter or confirm such as title, merchant, dates, category, and notes, are used to provide record storage, search, categorization, expiration reminders, and record packs. Text recognition and voice search are performed on device. We do not receive or retain your images, PDFs, extracted text, or audio.

2. Local storage and iCloud sync

Everstub stores record content on your device first. If you enable iCloud on your device, Everstub may use Apple’s private iCloud / CloudKit database to sync records across your own devices. Your Apple Account and Apple’s services manage synced data. We do not operate a server that can read your records.

You can delete records in the app and manage sync and storage in Apple ID / iCloud settings. Apple’s then-current system behavior governs how deleting local content or disabling iCloud affects synced content.

3. System permissions

  • Camera and Photos: Used only when you choose to capture or import material to create a record.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: Used only when you use voice search. Audio is not uploaded by the app.
  • Notifications: Used only with your permission to remind you about approaching expiration dates.

You can view or change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Purchases

Everstub may offer a one-time purchase. Apple’s App Store and StoreKit process payment. We do not receive your payment-card information.

5. Processing we do not perform

Everstub does not show ads, track you across apps or websites, sell personal information, or integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-collection services.

6. Policy updates

If the app’s features or information practices materially change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Please review the current version before using a new release.

7. Contact us

For questions about this policy, email andyzheung@aliyun.com. Do not email identity documents, full policy numbers, payment-card information, or other sensitive record content.
