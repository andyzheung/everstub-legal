Common questions

Why can’t I use the camera, import photos, or search by voice?

Open Everstub in iPhone Settings and allow the relevant Camera, Photos, Microphone, or Speech Recognition permission. Everstub requests a permission only when you use the related feature.

Do I need to review OCR results?

Yes. Check and edit extracted information before saving. OCR is there to reduce typing, not to make decisions for you.

Where are my records stored?

Records are stored on your device first. If you enable iCloud, syncing uses your private iCloud data space. Everstub does not operate a server that can read your records.

How do I delete records or manage synced data?

You can delete records in the app. Manage iCloud sync and storage in iOS Apple ID / iCloud settings.

How do I restore my lifetime purchase?

Open the Pro screen in the app and choose Restore Purchases. The App Store handles restoration and you will not be charged again.
